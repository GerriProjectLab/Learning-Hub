# Gerri Project Lab — Learning Hub

A place to share practical projects, experiments, and lessons—and learn from what others are building.

The aim is to make our work useful to someone else: explain the problem, share the approach, report what happened, and identify what we would do differently.
## Guides
- [Project Sharing Template](project-sharing-template.md) — Introduce your project, share lessons, and ask for feedback.
- [Stateful Project Practices](stateful-project-practices.md) — Preserve approved work, control changes, and maintain continuity.
## Areas of Interest

- **AI-assisted project work:** Prompts, workflows, and ways to produce reliable results with fewer iterations.
- **Stateful project practices:** Preserving approved work, documenting decisions, controlling changes, and maintaining a path back when something fails.
- **Photo and media organization:** Methods for inventorying scattered collections, identifying duplicates, and organizing an archive.
- **Website development:** Lessons from the Pelage website project, including design, content, usability, SEO, and verification.
- **3D design and printing:** Replacement parts, prototypes, print settings, and lessons from testing.

These are starting topics. Contributions about other practical projects are welcome.

## Share What You’re Doing

Open the **Discussions** tab to introduce yourself, share an experiment, ask a question, or offer feedback.

A useful project update answers:

1. What are you trying to accomplish?
2. What have you tried?
3. What worked—and what did not?
4. What evidence supports your conclusions?
5. What feedback or help would be useful?

Work in progress is welcome. You do not need a finished project to contribute.

## How We Work

- Establish the current baseline before making changes.
- Keep changes focused on the intended goal.
- Preserve unrelated work and decisions.
- Verify results before calling something complete.
- Record important decisions and why they were made.
- Distinguish tested findings from assumptions and ideas.
- Share reusable methods while keeping personal and confidential information private.

## Building This Hub

We are starting with project practices and case studies, then adding links to individual project repositories as they become ready to share.
