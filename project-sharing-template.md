# Project Sharing Template

Copy this template into a Discussion to introduce your project.
Include whatever is useful; unfinished work is welcome.

## Project
What are you building, and who is it for?

## Goal
What problem are you trying to solve?
What would success look like?

## Current Status
What works now? What remains unfinished?

## Approach
What tools, methods, or materials are you using?
Link to code, designs, or examples where available.

## What I Learned
- What worked?
- What failed or surprised me?
- What would I do differently?

Separate tested results from assumptions.

## Feedback Wanted
What specific question could others help you answer?

## Next Step
What will you try next?
